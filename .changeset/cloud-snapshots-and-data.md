If you signed in with "Continue with Saleor Cloud", Configuration now has a "Snapshots and data" page, also found by searching settings for "snapshot", "restore" or "clear database". It lists the environment's snapshots and lets you create, restore and delete them, restore from any snapshot in your organization, replace the data with a sample store, or clear its orders or its whole database. Destructive actions ask you to type the environment's name. Actions your Saleor Cloud role doesn't allow, or that must wait for a running task, are disabled with the reason. Previously this was only possible in the Saleor Cloud console.

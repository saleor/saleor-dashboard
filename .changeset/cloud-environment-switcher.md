If you signed in with "Continue with Saleor Cloud", the sidebar now has an environment switcher next to the Saleor logo. It opens on the environments of your current project. Use the back arrow to pick another project, and again to pick another organization; levels with only one option are skipped. Click an environment to open its Dashboard, or Cmd/Ctrl-click to open it in a new tab. The "Manage in Saleor Cloud" item replaces the cloud icon that appeared when you hovered over the logo. Users who sign in any other way see no change.

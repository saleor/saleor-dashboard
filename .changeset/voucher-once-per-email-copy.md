The voucher usage limit labelled "One use per customer" is now labelled "One use per email address", and its description says that the same person can redeem again with a different address. The limit has always been checked against the email address on the checkout or order, not against a person or an account, so the old wording promised more than the setting does. The description now also points to a total limit or one-use codes for capping redemptions, and the setting has moved below "One use per code" so that the two limits that do cap redemptions come first. How vouchers are applied has not changed.

The voucher usage limit labelled "One use per customer" is now labelled "One use per email address", and its description says that the same person can redeem again with a different address. The limit has always been checked against the email address on the checkout or order, not against a person or an account, so the old wording promised more than the setting does. The description now also points to a total limit or one-use codes for capping redemptions. How vouchers are applied has not changed.

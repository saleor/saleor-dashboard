Apps can now mark themselves as deprecated, and the dashboard shows it. Installed extensions shows a "Deprecated" badge and the app's reason under its row. The app's manage page and app page show the same notice. Installing a deprecated app shows the reason and asks you to confirm before Install is enabled. Deprecated apps keep working as before. Requires Saleor 3.23.38 or later.

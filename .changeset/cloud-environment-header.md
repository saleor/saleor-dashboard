If you signed in with "Continue with Saleor Cloud", the sidebar now shows the current environment's name in place of "Saleor Dashboard", with a Production or Sandbox badge and its project and organization below. The switch button next to it opens a dialog that lists all your environments grouped by project, with search, production environments marked, and the current one selected. Previously the sidebar only showed a small menu button, and you had to step through organizations and projects to find an environment.

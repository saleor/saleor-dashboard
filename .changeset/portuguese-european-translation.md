The Portuguese interface is now fully translated into European Portuguese. Many screens, including warehouses, channels, orders, and the rich-text editor, previously showed English text when Portuguese was selected; every message now has a Portuguese translation. Existing Portuguese wording now uses European terms, for example "palavra-passe" instead of "senha" and "morada" instead of "endereço". Counts of zero use the plural ("0 produtos" instead of "0 produto"), and numbers, prices, and dates follow European Portuguese formatting (for example "12 345,60 €"). Brazilian Portuguese keeps its own translation.

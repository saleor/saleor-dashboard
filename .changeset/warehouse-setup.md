A warehouse can be set up from its own page: assign channels, link shipping zones that share a channel, and follow the setup checklist until the warehouse can sell stock. Pickup is now a single "Offer pickup" setting that replaces public and private stock. The warehouse list shows whether its stock can be sold, the city and country, and a pickup label when pickup is on. New warehouses are created from the list, and deleting one names the stock and channels it removes. The shipping zone page only offers warehouses that share one of its channels, and removing a channel that would unlink warehouses from shipping zones asks for confirmation first.

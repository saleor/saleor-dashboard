The description of the Company Information section in Settings → Store no longer mentions an email address. The card has no email field; the sentence was left over from a mailing section removed in 2021. It also no longer says the address is used to calculate shipping rates, which Saleor doesn't do. It now says what the card holds and where it shows up: storefronts and apps can show it, and the Customer Emails app uses the address in email footers unless the app's own branding sets a different one.

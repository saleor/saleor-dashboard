The main navigation adapts to the screen width. On desktop, you can collapse the sidebar to an icon rail with the button next to the logo or with Cmd/Ctrl+B, and the dashboard remembers your choice. In the rail, every icon has a tooltip, and sections open their pages in a flyout. On tablets, the rail is always shown so pages keep their width, and the logo opens the full navigation over the page. On phones, an app bar with the menu button, logo, and search replaces the narrow column that held only the menu button. The navigation drawer slides in, can be swiped closed, closes after you follow a link, and has larger touch targets. "Go to Saleor Cloud" moved from a hover-only icon in the sidebar header to the account menu.
